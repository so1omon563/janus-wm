# Visual Language

This document records the current Janus visual direction. The canonical brand
source remains [docs/brand.md](../brand.md).

## Confirmed Direction

Janus should feel thoughtful, architectural, calm, native, and approachable. Its
visual language should support the idea of moving naturally between floating
and managed workflows without making the product feel technical or aggressive.

## Themes

- Gateway and threshold.
- Architecture and crafted objects.
- Duality, reflection, and balance.
- Flow between window-management modes.
- Travertine warm grey and calm material influence.
- Retro-futurist and mid-century tone without nostalgia becoming the point.

## Avoid

- Hacker aesthetics.
- Gaming aesthetics.
- Neon colors.
- Aggressive productivity branding.
- Overly technical terminology.
- Visual clutter.
- Literal Roman imagery, faces, columns, or ancient architecture.
